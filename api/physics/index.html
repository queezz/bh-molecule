    Classes¶

    BHModel ¶

    BH spectroscopy model. See Also Physics explainer

    Parameters:

    Name Type Description Default
    v00_wl_df DataFrame

    0-0 line positions (columns J, P, Q, R in nm); default from resources.

    		 None
    upper_energies (huber_herzberg, fernando_bernath)

    Upper A-state term values used in the rotational population weights. "huber_herzberg" (default) is :meth:energy with BH_A. "fernando_bernath" is Fernando & Bernath (1991) Eq. (1) with the per-level Table III constants BH_A_FB91, on the f level for Q lines and the e level for P/R lines.

    		 "huber_herzberg"
    v_max int

    Highest upper vibrational level summed by :meth:spectrum (when its own v_max is not given) and hence by :meth:full_fit_model. v_max=0 is the 0-0-only model.

    		 2
    band_positions (v00, own)

    "v00" (default) puts the lines of every band at the 0-0 positions. "own" uses each band's measured positions (F&B Table I, resources 11BH_v11.csv, 11BH_v22.csv) and skips lines not observed there.

    		 "v00"
    T_vib float or None

    None (default): every band is normalised at its own N = 1 level, with no vibrational population factor. A value in K weights band v by exp(-(T_v - T_0) / (k T_vib)) with the F&B band origins T_v.

    		 None
    baseline (constant, linear, quadratic, hgamma, fixed)

    "constant" (default): the baseline is the constant base. "linear": base + base_slope * (x - BASELINE_REF_NM) on the measured (unshifted) wavelength grid; adds the fit parameter base_slope [1/nm]. "quadratic": the linear form plus base_curv * (x - BASELINE_REF_NM)**2; adds base_slope and base_curv [1/nm^2], the curvature bounded to +-5 /nm^2. "hgamma": the linear form plus base_hg * L(x + dx), the far wing of H-gamma (:meth:hgamma_wing: a Lorentzian at HGAMMA_AIR_NM with HWHM HGAMMA_WING_HWHM_NM, 1 at 433.80 nm); adds base_slope and base_hg (bounds [0, 10]). The wing is convex and rises toward H-gamma only, unlike the symmetric quadratic. "fixed": a continuum computed before the fit and held: the model adds :meth:continuum_on (the array given to :meth:set_continuum, interpolated onto the measured grid) and holds base at 0 (:attr:fixed_params); no continuum parameter is fitted. BHFitter takes a continuum callable that sets it per spectrum.

    		 "constant"
    h2_lines (False, True, R2)

    True adds the two H2 Y 1 -> 2B 0 lines of H2_NUISANCE_LINES_NM as free-amplitude Gaussians with the BH line width, on the same shifted grid x + dx as the BH lines; adds the fit parameters I_H2_R01 and I_H2_R2. "R2" adds the resolved R2 line only (I_H2_R2), for fits that exclude the R0/R1 region from the data instead (BHFitter(exclude_nm=...)).

    		 False
    h2_r01_ratio float or None

    Only with h2_lines=True. None (default): the two H2 amplitudes are free. A positive value ties them: the R0/R1 line (433.2619 nm) gets h2_r01_ratio * I_H2_R2, so only I_H2_R2 is fitted. R0/R1 sits on BH Q(17) and the low-N Q lines and cannot be separated from them by the fit; the tie takes its amplitude from the resolved R2 line. DIEKE_H2_R01_OVER_R2 (1.29/1.20) is Dieke's ratio.

    		 None
    isotopologue ('11BH', natural)

    "natural" adds 10BH at its natural share (BH10_FRACTION = 0.199 of BH, i.e. 0.248 of 11BH), at the same T_rot: the 10BH (0-0) Q lines with their own f-level energies, each isotopologue normalised by its own rotational partition function, and the 10BH (1-1) R(7)/R(8) lines at 0.248 times the free 11BH R(7)/R(8) amplitudes (F&B Table V positions). No extra fit parameters. Needs upper_energies="fernando_bernath" and v_max=0.

    		 "11BH"
    bh10_positions (fb91, mass_scaled)

    10BH Q-line positions and energies with isotopologue="natural" (:mod:bh_molecule.isotopes): F&B Table V where measured and their Table VII constants elsewhere, or the mass-scaled 11BH Dunham set. They differ for Q(13) and above (see the module).

    		 "fb91"
    extra_lines tuple of {"F1", "F3"}

    Unidentified window features of EXTRA_LINES_NM added as free-amplitude Gaussians with the BH line width on the shifted grid, like the H2 lines; adds I_F1, I_F3 (bounds [0, 1]).

    		 ()
    rot_components (1, 2)

    2 makes the upper-state population a sum of two Boltzmann distributions, (1 - frac2) n(T_rot) + frac2 n(T_rot2), each normalised by its partition function, scaled so that frac2 = 0 is the one-temperature model with the same C. Adds T_rot2 [K] and frac2. Needs v_max=0.

    		 1
    r11_lines (free, tied)

    The 11BH (1-1) R(7) and R(8) lines in the window. "free" (default): two free amplitudes I_R7, I_R8 at R11_MODEL_NM. "tied": their areas follow from the common T_rot and the Hoenl-London factors of the (1-1) band (:meth:r11_tied_areas: upper e levels N' = 8, 9 of v' = 1), with one free population scale S_v1 = n(v'=1, N'=1) / n(v'=0, N'=1), at F&B Table I positions (433.644051 / 433.331292 nm, the 0.0037 nm offset of R11_MODEL_NM removed). I_R7 and I_R8 stay in the signature but are ignored, and :attr:fixed_params holds them at 0 in the fit. Needs v_max=0 and rot_components=1.

    		 "free"
    stretch bool

    True adds a linear wavelength stretch stretch (eps, dimensionless): the model lines sit on x + dx + eps * (x - STRETCH_REF_NM) instead of x + dx, i.e. a relative dispersion error of the measured grid. The Gaussian width is scaled by 1 + eps with it, so the line width on the measured grid stays w_inst (the instrument function is fixed in pixels) and only the positions move. The baseline stays on the measured grid.

    		 False
    Notes

    Extra fit parameters are appended to the production signature of :meth:full_fit_model in the order of :attr:extra_param_names. The defaults reproduce the production model exactly.

    hgamma_wing(xs: np.ndarray, power: float = HGAMMA_WING_POWER) -> np.ndarray staticmethod ¶

    Far wing of H-gamma for baseline="hgamma" on the rest-frame grid xs.

    :math:L(d) = [1 + (d/\gamma)^p]^{-1} / [1 + (d_0/\gamma)^p]^{-1} with :math:d = |x_s - \lambda_{H\gamma}|, :math:\gamma = HGAMMA_WING_HWHM_NM, :math:d_0 = HGAMMA_WING_REF_NM and :math:p = power (2: Lorentzian); 1 at 0.25 nm from the line centre.

    q_band(xs: np.ndarray, T_rot: float, w_inst: float, T_tra: float = 0.0) -> np.ndarray ¶

    r11_tied_areas(C: float, T_rot: float, S_v1: float) -> tuple[float, float] ¶

    Areas of the (1-1) R(7) and R(8) lines for r11_lines="tied".

    In the units of :meth:full_fit_model (the Q branch is 1e8 * :meth:spectrum): 1e8 C S_v1 g (2N'+1) exp(-dF / k T_rot) A(1, N', N'') h nu / 4 pi with N' = N'' + 1, dF the upper e-level energy above v' = 1, N' = 1 (upper_energies), and :meth:A_coeff's R-branch Hoenl-London factor (N' + 1)/2 and v' = 1 band coefficient. S_v1 is therefore n(v'=1, N'=1) / n(v'=0, N'=1).

    rot_partition(T_rot: float, isotope: str = '11BH', N2_max: int = 22) -> float ¶

    Rotational partition sum of the Q-branch upper levels (v' = 0, f), :math:Z = \sum_{N=1}^{N_2^{max}} (2N+1)\,e^{-\Delta F_N / k T}, with :math:\Delta F_N above N = 1, as :meth:spectrum weights them.
